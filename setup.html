CivicSays runs on Supabase. Paste your project credentials once and you're set.

The anon public key, not the service role key.
Don't have a project? Create one → Setup guide
Why is this needed?

CivicSays stores all data in your own Supabase project — tickets, comments, inquiries, and chat messages. You keep full ownership. The connection credentials are saved only in this browser's localStorage and are never sent anywhere except Supabase.
