How it works

From a problem to a closed ticket
in three steps.

1

Submit your request

Tell us what's happening. Add a photo or video link if it helps explain the issue. Pick "Request" for general services or "Complaint" if something's broken or wrong.

2

Get a tracking ID

We'll give you a unique code like CIV-AB3K9X. Save it. That's how you'll check status, add comments, and continue the conversation.

Common questions

Before you file.

Is CivicSays really free?
Yes — for residents, for governments, and forever. The platform runs on free-tier infrastructure and is open source. There is no paid tier, no upsell, and no "premium version."
Do I need to create an account?
No. Residents stay anonymous. The only credentials you need to find and comment on your ticket are the tracking ID plus the name and phone number you used at submission. Officials do log in — they're a known, accountable group.
What if I lose my tracking ID?
The ID is shown once at submission and you should save it. If you lose it, we can't look it up for you without an account system — so save it somewhere safe (a screenshot, a note on your phone, an email to yourself). It's short and easy to remember.
What types of issues can I report?
Anything your local government handles: broken streetlights, potholes, trash pickup, water issues, permits, noise complaints, parks, public safety concerns, and general service requests. For emergencies, always call your local emergency number — CivicSays is not a replacement for 911 or equivalent.
How long until I hear back?
That depends on the department and the issue. You'll see an acknowledgement from an official when the ticket is reviewed. You can comment any time to ask for an update, or use the "Ask a Question" live chat for faster responses on simple matters.
Is my personal information safe?
Your contact info is shared with the officials who handle your ticket — that's how they reach you. The data lives in your own Supabase project (which your municipality controls), is protected by row-level security, and is never sold or shared with third parties.
